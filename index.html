SOIC Drive Sync is a personal script that organizes accessible SOIC membership transcripts and attachments in the account owner’s Google Drive, and uses SOIC announcement emails to help identify session dates.

This is an independent personal tool. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with SOIC or Google. This public website provides information only; it does not display course content, email, credentials, or the private archive.
01

Organize accessible lessons

The script saves displayed transcripts and available lesson resources using the owner’s normal membership access. It does not download protected video streams or bypass access controls.

02

Identify session dates

Read-only Gmail access finds SOIC announcement and recording notices. Explicit session dates are matched conservatively to lessons; uncertain matches stay blank.

03

Maintain a private tracker

The script updates an ordered archive and spreadsheet in the owner’s selected Google Drive folder. A scheduled GitHub Actions job can check for new information.

Personal access, controlled by the owner

The owner authorizes Gmail and Drive separately through Google. Access can be revoked through Google Account settings. The public website has no sign-in form and does not collect Google account credentials.
